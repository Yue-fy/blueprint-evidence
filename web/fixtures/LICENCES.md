# Licences of the drawings named in these fixtures

Junction 2026 dataset. The drawing files are not in this repository. Questions and data derived from a drawing
keep that drawing's licence; our answers are MIT. **Entries about CubiCasa5K plans are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: for
non-commercial use only** (kept for the non-commercial Junction challenge, cleared with Stoa). Each such entry
carries a `"licence"` field saying so. The CubiCasa5K repository states CC BY-NC 4.0, its Zenodo record CC BY-NC-SA
4.0; the dataset applies the stricter one. Generated from the dataset's `data/<source>/LICENSE.md` tables.

second DfE level and our simulated scan of the DfE sheet. They are not in this repository: put the files in `samples/examples/` (or a folder named by `STOA_EXAMPLES_DIR`); see `samples/examples/README.md` for where each one comes from.
