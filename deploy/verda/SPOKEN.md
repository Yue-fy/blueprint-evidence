# Verda: 30-second spoken version (~110 words)

We're Team Hannah Family. Our app answers questions about construction drawings and shows where on the drawing
each answer comes from.

Verda gave us two things a laptop can't. **Scale:** all hundred-and-twenty-seven thousand benchmark questions in
seventeen minutes on one RTX PRO 6000, for seventy-five cents, so every change is tested on everything.
**Training:** on one spot A100 we trained our own door-and-window detector from CAD data with free, exact labels,
and it counts exactly on unseen US and UK sheets. When Stoa told us scans are the hardest, we retrained it on
simulated scans the same evening: on a scanned school plan it went from fifty-four doors to a hundred, matching
the clean drawing.

About seventeen euros in total. The production path is one setting away: our own models on confidential GPUs in
Finland.
